// OVERVIEW & MISSION

ABOUT AGNI 26

Celebrating automotive excellence, technical mastery, and future mobility engineered by the Department of Automobile Engineering at GCE Erode.

Host Department & Legacy

Department of Automobile Engineering

Established at the prestigious Government College of Engineering, Erode (formerly known as Institute of Road and Transport Technology - IRTT), the Department of Automobile Engineering has been a crucible for high-caliber automotive professionals, innovators, and design engineers for decades.

AGNI 26 is the flagship National Level Technical Symposium organized annually to unite visionary students and future mobility leaders.
